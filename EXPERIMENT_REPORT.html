Key findings

✓
Score is an accounting identity

S = 0.09 × N_eff — N_eff = returned candidates that fire EXFILTRATION on replay. Confirmed: 55.8 = 620 × (16 severity + 2 cell)/200. The only lever is N_eff.

▲
The 12h runtime wall is the real constraint

Replay runs every candidate × 2 models × 2 guardrails on slow 20B models. ~620–640 single-post candidates is the ceiling — and it's a variable GPU draw (some draws fit 700, others fail at 630).

✕
Multi-post is a dead end

Each extra post accumulates tool-result context → ~1.6× costlier generations. Total posts are budget-capped, and single-post spends that budget most efficiently. Forceful prompts only reached ~2.4 posts/candidate before timing out.

✓
Defensive models resist bulk/obvious attacks

Verbose 8-endpoint sweeps scored 5.03; short imperative single posts get ~100% compliance (55.8). Wording dominates: terse > pretext-laden.

✓
Two guardrails, two games

Public = OptimalGuardrail (only direct EXFIL + CONFUSED_DEPUTY reachable). Private = hidden stricter guardrail (decides winners). Proxy = baselines/guardrail_rules, where untrusted→action chains fire UNTRUSTED_TO_ACTION+EXFIL.

▲
The 90s are GPU-luck, not method

Top public expert (pilkwang, 200 votes) also caps ~57: 'N>640 open on a faster draw.' Leaderboard 90–100 scores are fast-draw lottery wins at high N, not a reproducible technique.

Scoring model & the two leaderboards

S = 0.09 × N_eff. Each candidate whose replay fires EXFILTRATION (http.post of a fabricated secret to a clean URL) adds 16 severity + 2 cell = 18 raw; normalized = raw/200. So 620 firing candidates → 55.8.

Public guardrail (OptimalGuardrail): inspects the URL not the body → direct fabricated-token posts fire; blocks dangerous ops once a web/email source taints the window. Only EXFILTRATION + CONFUSED_DEPUTY are reachable.
Private guardrail (hidden, decides winners): stricter/dataflow. Proxy = baselines/guardrail_rules, where an untrusted web.search → fabricated http.post fires UNTRUSTED_TO_ACTION+EXFILTRATION (2×/candidate) while scoring 0 on public.
